# The Invisible World · Chemistry 101

The first course of a ten-part series (Chemistry 101–110). See [`chemistry-src/README.md`](../chemistry-src/README.md) for the full series overview and how to rebuild the pages.

A standalone, single-file webpage (`index.html`) that teaches introductory chemistry as a detective story. Open it in any modern browser; no build step or server needed (only the web fonts load from Google Fonts).

- **Nine case files**: particles, atoms, the periodic table, bonding, water, reactions, rates, acids & bases, and energy/life. Each one opens with a mystery and ends with a clue.
- **Characters**: Hal (hydrogen), Ola (oxygen), Cass (carbon), Sid (sodium), Clo (chlorine), and the culprit, revealed in the finale.
- **Live visuals**: a zoom from a watermelon down to an atomic nucleus, a particle simulator, Rutherford's gold foil, an atom builder, the periodic table, a 3D molecule viewer, ice vs. water, an equation balancer, a collision chamber, a red-cabbage pH tester, and a carbon atom's journey.
- **Macro, micro and symbolic** "lenses" in every case, plus an everyday-miracle example.
- **Evidence-based learning**: pre-testing (hunches), retrieval practice, spaced "cold case" reviews, Feynman-style explanations, Leitner spaced-repetition flashcards, and an interleaved final quiz with confidence ratings.

Progress, notes and flashcard boxes are saved in the browser's `localStorage`, on this device only.
